## 1. What "passing" means for you

**Target: NCLC 7 or higher in all four skills.** This is the only entry rule for the French-language category draws. IRCC never averages the skills, so your **weakest skill decides** whether you qualify.
Source: [IRCC – Category-based selection](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/rounds-invitations/category-based-selection.html) (page modified 2026-06-22)

### TCF Canada score → NCLC (official IRCC table)

| NCLC | Listening (0–699) | Reading (0–699) | Writing (0–20) | Speaking (0–20) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10+ | 549–699 | 549–699 | 16–20 | 16–20 |
| 9 | 523–548 | 524–548 | 14–15 | 14–15 |
| 8 | 503–522 | 499–523 | 12–13 | 12–13 |
| **7 ← your minimum** | **458–502** | **453–498** | **10–11** | **10–11** |
| 6 | 398–457 | 406–452 | 7–9 | 7–9 |
| 5 | 369–397 | 375–405 | 6 | 6 |
| 4 | 331–368 | 342–374 | 4–5 | 4–5 |

Source: [IRCC – Language test results](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/language-requirements/language-testing.html). FEI's candidate manual maps **NCLC 7 = CEFR B2** in every skill.

**In plain English: you need a solid B2 in listening, reading, writing and speaking.** Every level decision in the app should target this.

**Practice target: NCLC 8.** Scores near a boundary can swing between sittings; FEI's manual says so explicitly. So the app should treat "just NCLC 7" in practice as *at risk*, not *safe*.

### What NCLC 7 is worth (CRS points)
From [IRCC – CRS criteria](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/check-score/crs-criteria.html):
- **+50 points** for NCLC 7+ in all four French skills *and* CLB 5+ in all four English skills.
- **+25 points** for NCLC 7+ in French with CLB 4 or lower in English, or no English test.
- If English is your first official language, French also earns **second-language points**: 3 per skill at NCLC 7–8 and 6 per skill at NCLC 9+, up to 22/24.
- ⚠️ **Action item:** if you don't already have a valid English test (IELTS General / CELPIP / PTE Core), taking one gets you the extra 25 points. That's cheap points for a Nigerian candidate.

### Recent French-category draws (context, not a promise)
- 2026 French draws cleared at **CRS ~382–420**, usually 4,000–5,000 invitations each and ~2 draws/month. The latest were 382 on 19 Aug 2026 and 391 on 6 Aug 2026. General draws were 500+.
- Cut-offs have been **falling** because the French-speaking pool is thinning.
- Sources: [moving2canada – French draws](https://moving2canada.com/immigration/express-entry/french-category-based-express-entry-draws/), [CIC News – pool running low on French speakers](https://www.cicnews.com/2026/05/the-express-entry-pool-is-running-low-on-french-speakers-what-does-it-mean-for-other-candidates-0574875.html), [immigration.ca – 6 Aug 2026 draw](https://immigration.ca/canada-invites-5000-french-speaking-candidates-express-entry-crs-391-august-6-2026/). Policy can change at any time. Check [IRCC rounds](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/rounds-invitations.html) monthly.
- Reminder: you must *also* qualify for an Express Entry program (FSW / CEC / FST): work experience, ECA, proof of funds and so on. The app doesn't cover this, but check it now. There's no point hitting NCLC 7 and then being ineligible for another reason.

## 2. Official exam format (TCF Canada)

| Section | Format | Time | Scored |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compréhension orale (listening) | 39 MCQ, 4 options, one listen only | **35 min** | 100–699 |
| Compréhension écrite (reading) | 39 MCQ, 4 options | **60 min** | 100–699 |
| Expression écrite (writing) | 3 tasks | **60 min total** | 0–20 |
| Expression orale (speaking) | 3 tasks, face-to-face with examiner, recorded | **12 min total** | 0–20 |

**No grammar/structures section** in TCF Canada, unlike TCF Tout Public. Grammar still matters because it's scored inside writing and speaking.

**Writing tasks** (word limits are strict):
1. Short message to describe, tell or explain: **60–120 words**
2. Article/letter/note to several readers, telling an experience and commenting: **120–150 words**
3. Text comparing two points of view, then defending your own: **120–180 words**

⚠️ A task that's missing, off-topic, or **outside the word range** can be marked "**A1 non atteint**". The app must enforce word counts.

**Speaking tasks:**
1. *Entretien dirigé* (guided interview), no prep: **2 min**. Talk about yourself.
2. *Exercice en interaction* (role-play) with **2 min prep**, then **3 min 30** of dialogue. You ask the examiner questions to get information in an everyday situation.
3. *Expression d'un point de vue* (giving your opinion), no prep: **4 min 30**. Argue a position.

**How it's scored:**
- Listening and reading scores are weighted by question difficulty. Questions rise in difficulty from A1 to C2 through the test, and wrong answers don't lose points, so **never leave a blank**.
- Writing and speaking are each marked by 2 independent graders. Each task gets a level from A1 to C2, and those levels combine into a /20 score.
- Grading criteria: *linguistic* (vocabulary range, grammar accuracy, spelling, fluency, pronunciation), *pragmatic* (coherence, cohesion, how well the topic is developed) and *sociolinguistic* (matching the register to the situation).

**Other rules:**
- Certificate is **valid 2 years**.
- **Retake:** wait at least **30 days**; you must redo all 4 sections; no limit on attempts.
- ⚠️ FEI **stopped accepting remark (appeal) requests from 1 Sept 2026** "until further notice". You can't count on an appeal.
- ⚠️ Some prep blogs claim "adaptive AI listening" and other 2026 changes. **No official source confirms this.** Ignore it unless FEI publishes it.

Sources:
- FEI *Manuel du candidat*, TCF Canada section ([PDF mirror hosted by AF Detroit](https://www.afdetroit.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Manuel_du_candidat_TCF.pdf))
- [AF Lagos – E-TCF Canada](https://www.afnigeria.org/lagos/tcf-canada/) (same format, confirmed for 2026)
- [FEI – TCF Canada](https://www.france-education-international.fr/test/tcf-canada). This page blocks automated readers, so open it in a browser to double-check before booking.

## 3. Realistic timeline — the honest version

| Level | Alliance Française cumulative *guided* hours |
|---|---|
| A1 | 60–100 |
| A2 | 160–200 |
| B1 | 360–400 |
| **B2** | **560–650** |

Source: [AF Leeds FAQ / AF CEFR guidance](https://afleeds.org.uk/faq). The FSI puts French at **600–750 class hours** to professional working level, and that assumes daily homework on top ([Hello French – FSI](https://www.hellofrench.com/en/tips/how-long-does-it-take-to-learn-french)).

Guided hours are teacher-led. For self-study, allow **roughly 900–1,100 total hours**, counting classes, SRS, listening and practice. You'll also need a B2 *in all four skills*, and speaking and writing are where self-learners fall behind.

**Your maths:**
- 4 h/day × 7 days = **28 h/week**.
- 1,000 hours ÷ 28 = **~36 weeks ≈ 8.3 months**.
- Starting 28 Sep 2026 → exam-ready around **early June 2027**.

**Verdict:** 10 months is **enough, but with little slack**. It only works if you:
1. Actually average ~28 h/week, weekends included. At 4 h on weekdays only (20 h/week), B2 in all skills realistically slides to **~11–12 months**, past your deadline.
2. Get **real speaking practice with a human** from about month 3. Self-study alone rarely gets speaking to B2 in this time.
3. Sit the **first attempt in early June 2027**, leaving room for **one retake** before 23 July: results take about 15 working days, then the 30-day wait.

**Shortest safe timeline:** about **8 months** (first sitting early June 2027). Going faster would need 5–6 h/day. Even then, under ~7 months to B2 in all four skills from zero is unusual. Plan for June and treat anything earlier as a bonus.

## 6. What this changes about the app (research-driven)
1. **Four skills, no grammar section.** Level estimates are shown per skill as **NCLC**, plus CEFR.
2. **The weakest skill is what matters.** "Would I pass today?" = min(skill NCLC) ≥ 7 with a margin.
3. **Enforce word counts** in writing (60–120 / 120–150 / 120–180) and warn loudly outside range.
4. **Speaking uses the exact real timings:** 2:00 / (2:00 prep + 3:30) / 4:30.
5. **Listening plays each audio once** in exam mode, like the real test.
6. **Offline-first (PWA).** Nigerian power and data are unreliable; the app must work without a connection and cache content.
7. **Booking reminders** at weeks 20 and 25, plus a retake-buffer countdown.
8. **English test nudge** for the extra 25 CRS points if you don't have CLB 5+ English.
